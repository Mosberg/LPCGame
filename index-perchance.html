The world beneath the ash

Every journey starts with a seed.

The overworld, dungeon, terrain, props and effects are painted at runtime. A fixed seed means the Vale is consistent for every adventurer — while still feeling alive through its day, night and weather cycles.

  • Procedural overworld Biomes, roads, ruins, resource nodes, a walled village and a hidden cave entrance.
  • The Emberdeep dungeon Rooms, corridors, braziers and a boss hall beneath a key-locked gate.
  • A shared world, optionally Other heroes may wander beside you as colourful echoes with names and chat bubbles.
